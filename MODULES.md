# Research Modules

## 1. Evgeny's Theorem

Fractal / Sierpiński-gasket trace-defect construction.

Principal identity:

$$
\Delta_m(H^4,\theta)=-16(3^{m-1}+1)\sin^2\!\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right).
$$

Current focus: complete operator-level Lean proof (base case $m=1$ done; general $m$ in progress).

Repository: [Evgeny-Theorem](https://github.com/RFT-SIRM/Evgeny-Theorem)

## 2. SU(2) / Operator Layer

Hilbert-space indexing, Pauli-axis rotations, edge transport, connection operators, unitary constructions.

Formalization proceeds from unitarity and diagonal identities toward the diagonal of $H^2$, trace identities, and the final defect formula.

## 3. Yang–Mills

SU(2) gauge structure, holonomy, continuum scaling, transfer/Hamiltonian constructions, mass-gap-related questions.

Exact local identities are recorded separately from continuum QFT claims.

## 4. Riemann / Hilbert–Pólya

Candidate self-adjoint spectral constructions and arithmetic trace / prime-orbit structures.

Successful matches and mismatches are both recorded.

## 5. BSD

Local SU(2) representation associated with elliptic-curve Frobenius data:

$$
\cos\theta_p = \frac{a_p}{2\sqrt{p}},
\qquad
U_p = \mathrm{diag}(e^{i\theta_p}, e^{-i\theta_p}).
$$

The global rank relation remains a separate open problem.

## 6. Navier–Stokes

Invariant / transition structure of incompressible flow, especially energy balance and divergence-free constraints.

Numerical diagnostics are kept distinct from regularity proofs.

## 7. P vs NP / Scheduling

Transaction conflicts as graph structure; admissibility, bounded execution, invariants, combinatorial complexity.

Application of the framework — not a claim that P vs NP is resolved.

## 8. Phi-Genesis / Fractal Spectral Module

Spectral decimation, fractal Weyl behavior, gaps, twists, candidate fermion-mass selection on the Sierpiński gasket.

Repository: [Phi-Genesis](https://github.com/RFT-SIRM/Phi-Genesis)

Verified, rejected, and unresolved claims are preserved separately.
